# Rebrief template

## Current understanding

Explain the user, current problem, desired outcome, main journey, evidence-backed scope, proposed additions and the most material limitation in 5-8 plain-language lines.

## Supplied inputs

| Input | What it confirms | Read/version/date | Limit | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Distinguish documents, actual samples, design drafts, API claims, inspected source and observed runtime behavior.

## Current and expected behavior

| Area | Current | Expected | Confirmed/Proposed/Assumed/Unknown | Source/owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Include a diagram only when useful. Label verified/current facts separately from proposed topology or assumptions. Unknown systems remain unknown.

## Material questions

| Question or conflict | Recommendation/options | Impact | Evidence/decision needed | Owner | Dependent step |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Lead with 3-5 questions that matter to the stated outcome. Check applicable business rules, permissions, lifecycle, data quality, integration failures, migration, money, privacy, AI proof and operations. Do not add irrelevant blockers.

## Next step and source receipt

State what can proceed, what depends on missing evidence and why. Record source read boundaries and material deltas. A rebrief is not a promise of budget, date, accuracy or development readiness.
