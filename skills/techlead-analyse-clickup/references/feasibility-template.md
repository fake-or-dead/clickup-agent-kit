# Feasibility template

## Recommendation

State feasible now, feasible with conditions or blocked for the named scope. Give the reason and source boundary.

## Approach and alternatives

Describe systems, data, permissions, integration and proposed deployment. Label diagrams current/verified or proposed/assumed. Compare custom, purchased or mixed approaches only when useful.

## Workforce and costs

Record hours per person-day, rate, currency, tax basis and timezone as supplied project inputs. Leave absent values unknown. Do not reuse historical example estimates as a benchmark.

| Workstream | Owner | One-time effort range | Basis | Recurring work | Included/shared work |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Separate development, product, design, QA, data, operations, security, documentation and client preparation where relevant. Reconcile totals and transfers. Person-days are not calendar duration.

For paid services, show measured or assumed usage units, current official price/date/source, setup/backfill, hosting, tools, people, reserve and recurring totals. If verification is unavailable, label costs unverified rather than inventing current prices.

For AI analysis, separate source events from analysis units. Include representative samples, definitions, a human truth set, review/adjudication, holdout evaluation and ongoing review. Model confidence is not measured accuracy. Keep human effort separate from token charges.
